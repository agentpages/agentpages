Add comments to the Store. `comments_list`, `comments_reply` and `comments_resolve` are tools of an agent, and with `comments_start` operations a host runs for a viewer by the `url` they present. Every Store has them: they are no feature. `storeSuite` gains the core cases of comments, which start threads through `host` and need a `maxThreadEntries` of at least 3.
