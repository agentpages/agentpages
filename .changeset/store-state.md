Add state and identity to the Store, without trees. The Store answers `state_get`, `state_list`, `state_commit` and `state_commits`, and gains `view`, which answers the same four as methods of a view, and `identity/get`, for the page behind the link the host holds. A commit to the page scope of a typed page is checked against the schemas of its type version. `pages_publish` refuses a change of a page's kind or `typeId`, and `types_publish` decides a change of what is a tree before a stale `baseVersion`. A tool or method exists exactly when the Store advertises its feature. `createStore` takes an `actorKey` of at least 32 bytes: the `id` of a viewer is the HMAC-SHA-256 of the page and the subject under it, so it differs from page to page. A repeat with the same params gets its first result before the limits, and an `opId` used with other params is refused after them. The Store keeps the 1024 compiled schemas it used last. The conformance `Driver` gains `view`, a viewer can have a `name`, and `storeSuite` gains the cases of state, identity and typed pages, with one that plays random commits against a model. It picks the cases from the capability of the Store: for each of `state` and `identity`, what the feature does or that it is not there. The function that makes a driver is no longer async.
